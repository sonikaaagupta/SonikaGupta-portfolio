Frame — About

Background & design philosophy

SG
Sonika GuptaPune, IN

I'm a dedicated, detail-oriented UI/UX Designer and Developer with 4+ years of experience creating user-friendly web solutions — from first sketch to shipped interface.

I design in Figma and Adobe Photoshop, then build what I design with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap and React.js, Redux and Tailwind CSS — so nothing gets lost in handoff, because I'm usually the one doing both sides of it.

I care about balancing creativity with function: a screen should look considered and still hold up under real usage — clear hierarchy, accessible contrast, and layouts that don't fall apart on a small screen. I'm always learning, staying current with design and frontend trends so the work stays sharp.

"Good art is a talent. Good design is a skill." — a principle I design by: craft over chance.

01 — E-commerce · Personal project

Orgbeauty — a premium skincare experience

Role: UI/UX Design & Frontend Build · Figma → HTML · CSS · JS ·Bootstrap

ProblemMost template shopping UIs feel transactional and generic — they don't hold up next to how luxury brands present themselves elsewhere.
ResearchAudited luxury retail sites (Sephora, Dior, Chanel, Gucci). Observed consistent use of dark editorial photography, restrained typography, and generous whitespace.
DecisionsDesigned a dark luxury theme with a refined type scale, generous whitespace, and a curated product grid over a dense catalogue layout.
MockupBuilt the full moodboard and screens in Figma and Photoshop, then implemented the interface in React with Redux Toolkit managing cart and product state.
ResultA cohesive, boutique-feeling shopping flow — from static mockup to a working cart — delivered as a self-contained downloadable build.

02 — E-commerce · Personal project

E-Mart — a responsive marketplace

Role: UI/UX Design & Frontend Build · Figma → HTML5/CSS3/Bootstrap

ProblemA general marketplace needs to turn casual browsing into purchases across very different screen sizes, without the layout feeling like an afterthought on mobile.
ResearchReviewed navigation and filtering patterns on mainstream marketplaces to see what made scanning many products fast and low-friction.
DecisionsChose a consistent card grid, a sticky category nav, and a Bootstrap 5 breakpoint system so the layout reflows cleanly rather than just shrinking.
MockupDesigned every page in Figma and Photoshop, then converted the designs into HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and Bootstrap 5.
ResultA fully responsive storefront that holds its layout and legibility from desktop down to mobile.

03 — LUXE-Premium Shop · Personal project

Luxe- Premium Shop

Role: UI/UX Design & Frontend Build · Figma → React + Redux

ProblemLuxury e‑commerce often struggles to balance premium aesthetics with functional responsiveness. The challenge was to design a storefront that feels boutique and editorial.
ResearchNoted that dark photography, minimal typography, and curated grids improved product focus.
DecisionsAdopted a consistent card grid for product display. Applied a dark luxury theme with muted gold accents to reinforce premium branding.
MockupDesigned all pages in Figma and Photoshop, starting from wireframes to polished UI.Converted designs into HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap 5.
ResultDelivered a fully responsive storefront that maintains luxury aesthetics across devices. Improved product focus by reducing clutter and emphasizing curated grids.
